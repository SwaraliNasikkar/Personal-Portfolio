# 🎀 Personal Portfolio Website ✨
Hey there! I'm so happy you're here 💕 This is my personal portfolio website, a cozy little space on the internet where I showcase my creative work, development skills, and a bit of who I am 🌸
Built with love, attention to detail, and a soft aesthetic, this site is my digital resume, my creative outlet, and a welcoming place for anyone who wants to know more about me!

## 🌟 What’s Inside?


- Home: A warm welcome with soft colors, subtle animations, and a snapshot of me 💫

- About Me: A short intro with a dash of personality – who I am, what I love, and why I do what I do 💁🏻‍♀️

- Projects: A gallery of hand-picked personal and professional projects that I'm super proud of 💻

- Skills: A curated list of tools, technologies, and design goodies I use and adore 🎨

- Contact: Let’s connect! Whether it’s for collaboration, freelancing, or just to say hi, I’d love to hear from you 💌


## 🛠 Tech Stack:

- Frontend: React / HTML / CSS / JavaScript (or insert your stack!)

- Styling: Tailwind CSS / SCSS / Styled Components with a soft, feminine color palette 💗

- Animations: Framer Motion / AOS / GSAP for those cute and smooth transitions ✨
